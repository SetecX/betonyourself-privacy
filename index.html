Bet On Yourself — Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 October 2026

Bet On Yourself runs entirely on your iPhone. There is no account, no server, and nothing you do in the app is sent anywhere. Apple Health, Calendar and Reminders are read on your device only, and only for the features described below. The tokens in the app are imaginary and have no cash value.

What is collected

Nothing. The app has no account system, no sign-in, and no backend to send data to. It contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind, so it never asks for permission to track you across apps.

What is stored on your device

All of the following is saved inside the app's private storage on your iPhone and is never uploaded:

  • Your token balance, open tickets, settled history and saved goals.
  • Your daily check-in — the sleep, mood and energy sliders you set yourself. These are your own input, not Apple Health data, and they only adjust the odds you are offered.
  • Any notes you type when settling a goal by hand.
  • Cosmetics you have unlocked or bought with tokens, and your achievements.

Apple Health

Health is optional, and is used for goals that can be measured — steps, Move and Exercise, workouts, mindfulness, sleep and stand hours. Your reading for today decides two things: the odds a measurable goal is offered at, and whether it was met. The app asks only for read access and never writes anything to Health.

Permission is requested when you lock a goal that needs it, not when you first open the app. Goals settled on the honor system, and Focus timers, work without granting Health access at all. With permission granted, the app may check in the background and can mark a goal as won while it is closed — it never marks a goal as lost automatically, and missing Health data is treated as "cannot verify", never as a zero.

Calendar and Reminders

Also optional, and only when you tap Add from Calendar or Reminders. That copies today's event titles and times, and unfinished reminder titles, onto the board as draft goals for you to choose from. Attending an event or completing a reminder does not settle anything.

The app only reads. It never creates, edits or deletes your events or reminders. One thing worth explaining: iOS does not offer apps a read-only level of calendar access, so the permission sheet says Full Access even though this app never writes.

Notifications

All notifications are created and scheduled on your device — goal reminders, a nudge when a measured goal is met, and an alert when a Focus session finishes. There are no push notifications from a server, because there is no server. You are asked for permission only when you lock a goal that would use one.

Backups, export and deletion

Your data lives in the app's private storage. It is included in your iPhone backup, whether that is an iCloud Backup or a computer backup — that is Apple backing up your phone, not the app syncing anything anywhere. Deleting the app deletes its data.

You can export your whole account to a file from Settings, and restore one later. Where that file goes is entirely your choice, and once written it is an ordinary file you control — the app does not track it or reach it again. Restoring asks you to confirm, replaces what is currently in the app, and refuses files that are damaged or contain implausible values, leaving your existing data untouched.

If the app's own save file ever becomes unreadable, it is kept aside rather than deleted so that your history can be recovered. Those files live in the app's private storage too, and are removed when you delete the app.

Tokens

Tokens are a score, not money. They have no cash value, cannot be bought or sold, cannot be withdrawn, and cannot be exchanged for anything of value. There are no in-app purchases. Everything tokens buy is cosmetic.

What the app never asks for

  • Your location.
  • Photos, camera, microphone or contacts.
  • Clinical or medical records.

Children

Bet On Yourself is not directed at children. Although no real money is involved, the app is presented in the style of a sportsbook, and it is rated accordingly on the App Store.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour changes in a way that affects anything above, this page is updated and the date at the top changes with it.

Support and contact
